Common Fixes
403 Error (API Key rejected)
Go to Google Cloud Console → Credentials → your API Key → Edit.
Under "Application restrictions" set to None. Under "API restrictions" make sure Google Sheets API is allowed.
404 Error (Sheet not found)
Double-check your Sheet ID — it's the part of the URL between /d/ and /edit.
Also confirm the sheet is shared publicly: Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer.
Data loads but shows 0s
Your sheet headers must be lowercase with underscores exactly as shown (e.g. live_price not Live Price).
Tab names must be exactly: Income, Expenses, Assets, NetWorth (capital first letter, no spaces).
Numbers have currency symbols or commas (€1,234.00)
Enter numbers as plain numbers in your sheet: 1234.00 not €1,234.00.
The app strips $ and , automatically but not € — format cells as "Number" not "Currency" in Google Sheets.
How to format cells correctly in Google Sheets
Select all data columns → Format → Number → Number (not Currency, not Automatic).
This ensures values come through as plain numbers via the API.

⚖ Margin Leverage Regime
FINRA customer margin debt · monthly · slow-moving context for deployment timing
No data
What this measures: how much money US investors have borrowed to buy stocks, relative to the market. When borrowing grows much faster than prices, a small drop can force margin calls and selling cascades. When borrowing has been flushed out, forced sellers are gone and entries are safer. It is regime context, not a timing trigger: data is month-end and published about 3 weeks late, and margin debt usually falls after prices do.
Add a Margin tab (at least 13 months) to your Google Sheet — setup steps below.

How to set up & update the data (monthly, ~3 min)
1. Source. FINRA Margin Statistics page: finra.org/rules-guidance/key-topics/margin-accounts/margin-statistics. FINRA updates it in the 3rd week of the following month. It has no data feed, so copy it by hand.
2. Create a tab named exactly Margin in your sheet, row 1 headers: month · debit_balances · free_credit_cash · free_credit_margin · spx_close
3. First time (seed history). On the FINRA page click Download the Data (Excel, from Jan 1997). Paste the three FINRA columns into columns B–D, oldest month first. 5+ years works; full history is best. Before 2010 FINRA gives one combined free-credit figure: put it in free_credit_cash and 0 in free_credit_margin.
4. Month column (A): type real dates like 2026-09-01 (first of the month). Keep FINRA's numbers as plain numbers in $ millions (format → Number).
5. spx_close (E), optional but recommended (unlocks the relative-leverage metrics). In E2 enter and fill down:
=LET(r,GOOGLEFINANCE("INDEXSP:.INX","close",EOMONTH(A2,0)-6,EOMONTH(A2,0)),INDEX(r,ROWS(r),2))
If older rows error, paste month-end closes manually. After filling, copy → paste values only.
6. Each month: add one new row at the bottom for the latest FINRA month, then press ↻ REFRESH. The sheet must stay shared as "Anyone with the link".

How to read this
A Buy Setup 9 means the market has closed lower than 4 bars earlier for 9 straight bars — classic bottoming exhaustion. A Sell Setup 9 is the mirror image at tops. The Countdown to 13 that follows is a stronger, secondary confirmation. Strict DeMark mode adds the bar-13 qualifier, TDST cancellation, setup perfection (★) and recycling; Original keeps the first version for comparison. This is a personal re-implementation (platforms differ on edge cases) — use the Backtest panel to see whether the signals beat chance on your data, and treat them as a probability tilt, not a standalone trade trigger.

Pattern Chart
How to read this. Two engines run side by side: Support Reversal (repeated swing-low floor plus reversal) and Harmonic (Gartley/Bat/Butterfly). Harmonic READY needs a valid pattern zone, a confirmation candle, 3 of 4 independent signal families (geometry is mandatory), and every gate passing. Earnings dates, options flow and dark-pool data are not checked. Check earnings yourself before buying. Google Sheet mode reads whichever ticker sits in PE_Data!B1 and builds the backtest record one ticker at a time. Backtest limits: your watchlist is today's stocks (survivorship bias flatters results), no fees or slippage, recovery-type setups excluded (washout comes from your sheet), and there is no out-of-sample split or per-layer ablation yet.
